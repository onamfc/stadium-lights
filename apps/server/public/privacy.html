Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Stadium Lights is a mobile application created and maintained by Site Transition. References to "we", "our", or "us" in this policy refer to Site Transition. This policy explains what information we collect through the Stadium Lights app and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Location Data: When you join a light show, we collect your GPS coordinates to determine your position in the venue and assign you to a zone. This allows the light patterns to synchronize based on where you're standing.
  • Device Identifier: We generate a random identifier stored on your device to maintain your session and allow you to rejoin groups.
  • Display Name: If you choose to set a display name, it is stored temporarily with your session.

How We Use Your Information

  • To assign you to a zone based on your location in the venue
  • To synchronize flashlight patterns with other participants
  • To allow controllers to see participant counts by zone (not individual locations)
  • To enable you to resume your session if you close and reopen the app

Data Retention

  • Location data is held in memory only during an active session and is not persisted to disk
  • Groups with random codes expire after 24 hours
  • Groups with custom codes expire after 7 days
  • When a group expires or is released, all associated participant data is deleted

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your location data is only used within the app to coordinate light shows.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data during transmission. Location data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Your Choices

  • You can deny location permission, though this will limit the app's functionality
  • You can leave a group at any time, which removes your data from that session
  • Uninstalling the app removes your device identifier

Children's Privacy

Stadium Lights does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or the Stadium Lights app, please contact Site Transition at: privacy@sitetransition.com
